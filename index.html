Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) Policy

Statutory Compliance Statement pursuant to the Protection of Personal Information Act, No. 4 of 2013 (POPIA), Republic of South Africa.

Key Architecture Guarantee — Client-Side Privacy: ChaoShield processes all uploaded PDF documents, bank receipts, and payment screenshots exclusively inside your local web browser's RAM. Document binaries and metadata are NEVER uploaded, stored, or indexed on external cloud servers.

1. Purpose and Scope

This policy applies to all users, sole proprietors, small-and-medium enterprises (SMEs), and organizations using the ChaoShield platform to detect fraudulent proof-of-payment (POP) notices, synthetic documents, and invoice manipulation. We adhere strictly to the conditions set out in Chapter 3 of the POPIA Act.

2. The 8 Lawful Processing Conditions under POPIA

  • Condition 1 — Accountability: ChaoShield maintains full governance over its client-side processing algorithms, ensuring that zero unauthorized interception or persistence of financial identifiers occurs during metadata extraction.
  • Condition 2 — Processing Limitation: Processing is strictly limited to extracting document structural markers (e.g., PDF creator software, creation timestamps, and digital certificate flags). Personal identifying information (such as account numbers or full names) is parsed ephemerally in-memory only to evaluate authenticity and is immediately purged upon browser session termination.
  • Condition 3 — Purpose Specification: Data messages and electronic receipts are processed exclusively for the purpose of fraud detection, forensic validation, and scam risk mitigation.
  • Condition 4 — Further Processing Limitation: Under no circumstances are document records, metadata headers, or financial details repurposed, sold, or shared with third-party advertising or analytics networks.
  • Condition 5 — Information Quality: We employ standard cryptographic hashing algorithms (SHA-256) to verify that the file being evaluated has not been tampered with or altered during transit.
  • Condition 6 — Openness & Transparency: Full technical documentation regarding our metadata detection rules (e.g., flagging Microsoft Word or Canva creators for alleged bank POPs) is made openly accessible to users.
  • Condition 7 — Security Safeguards: By leveraging local browser isolation (Web Crypto API, Canvas sandbox, and client-side PDF.js), the risk of cloud-based data breaches or unauthorized access is eliminated by design.
  • Condition 8 — Data Subject Participation: Users retain complete control over their local cryptographic registry. Users may at any time inspect stored hashes or clear their browser's local storage with a single click.

ChaoShield General Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Committed to Absolute Privacy & Data Minimization

Our Zero-Cloud Storage Guarantee: We do not maintain a remote database of uploaded bank receipts, invoices, or customer identities. Your files never leave your device.

1. What Information We Access and Process

When you drop or select a file into ChaoShield, the browser executes client-side JavaScript to inspect:

  • File Container & Format: Magic bytes (%PDF-1.x, PNG headers, JPEG EXIF data).
  • PDF Metadata Fields: /Creator, /Producer, /Author, /CreationDate, /ModDate, and /Title dictionaries.
  • Cryptographic Digest: Local SHA-256 computation to confirm integrity against known tampered documents.
  • Visible Document Text: Rendered client-side to verify financial entities (Bank name, settlement status, PayShap ISO 20022 clearing tokens).

2. Browser Local Storage (Cookies & Telemetry)

ChaoShield does NOT use tracking cookies or third-party profiling pixels. We utilize standard HTML5 Local Storage strictly for:

  • sentinel_free_scans_left: To keep track of your 5 free trial scans.
  • sentinel_is_subscribed: To remember your active SME (R50/mo) or Pro subscription state.
  • sentinel_sha256_registry_v2: Your local forensic registry of analyzed files so you can cross-examine duplicate uploads.

3. Electronic Evidence & ECTA Act Compliance

Under Section 15 and Section 86 of the South African Electronic Communications and Transactions Act (ECTA No. 25 of 2002), digital evidence derived from cryptographic hashes and unmodified metadata streams constitutes admissible evidence in commercial dispute resolution and criminal fraud investigations.

4. Your Rights as a Data Subject

You have the right to request access to any data you store locally, export your cryptographic registry, or immediately purge all stored records by clicking the "Reset Demo Scans" or "Clear Registry" buttons located on the platform.
